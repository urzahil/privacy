Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy applies to the Android apps published by urzahil.rocks ("we", "us"), including F1 Live Timing, Foodie, ConfNav and TaskFlow (the "apps").

Summary

We do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you enter or create in the apps stays on your own device.

Data we collect

None. The apps have no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

Data stored on your device

Settings and content you create in the apps are stored locally on your phone. If you have Android backup enabled, this data may be included in your Android backup to your own Google account. That backup is handled by Android and Google under your own account settings and Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Internet access

Some apps may connect to publicly available internet services to display up-to-date information (for example, motorsport timing and results). These requests do not include personal information from us, but, as with any internet connection, the service you connect to can see your device's IP address. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Third parties

We do not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties, because we do not have any.

Children

The apps do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
